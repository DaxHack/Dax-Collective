## SCRIPT — FINAL

There's a kind of tired that doesn't come from being lazy.
It comes from continuing when nothing seems to happen.

You're not imagining it.
The waiting is real.
The effort is real.
And the silence — that's real too.

But waiting isn't the same as standing still.
The fact that you're still here, still going — that matters.

Psalm 27 says: "Wait for God. Be strong, and let your heart take courage."
Not passive.
Just strength in the quiet.

You don't need to see it to keep going.
Start with today.

Share this with someone who needs it.
