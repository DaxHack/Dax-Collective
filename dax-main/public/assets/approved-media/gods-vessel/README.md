# Approved Media — God's Vessel

This folder stores manually-approved photo and video assets for the **God's Vessel** brand.

## What belongs here

- Product mockup photos of archive pieces (hoodies, tees, study cards)
- Flat-lay photography of apparel and accessories
- Typography and design detail shots
- Behind-the-scenes production and printing photos

## Naming convention

```
[product-type]-[name-of-god]-[view].[ext]
```

Examples:
- `hoodie-el-shaddai-front.jpg`
- `study-card-adonai-detail.jpg`
- `tee-elohim-flatlay.jpg`

## Privacy rules

- No photos of identifiable people wearing product without their written consent on file
- Assets must be set to `usageStatus: "Approved"` and `privacyStatus: "Public Safe"` in `approvedMedia.js`
- Mockup assets without people are preferred for initial approvals

## Content series tags

Use one of these `relatedSeries` values:

- `Names of God: The Dictionary Series`
- `Archive Notes`
