## MANUAL STEPS FOR ME

These cannot be done by AI tools. You must do them yourself.

1. **Create all Google accounts** — One Google account per YouTube channel (or use brand channels under a Google Brand Account). Recommended: create one main Google account, then add Brand Accounts for each channel.
2. **Claim YouTube handles** — Go to youtube.com/handle after creating each channel.
3. **Create TikTok accounts** — Set each to Creator account (not personal) after creation.
4. **Create Instagram accounts** — Switch each to Professional/Creator account after creation.
5. **Create Facebook Pages** — Must be Pages (not personal profiles). Choose Creator or Media/Entertainment category.
6. **Add link-in-bio** — Set up a Linktree (or similar) for The Dax Collective Instagram that points to all sub-brands. Add the link to the Instagram bio.
7. **Add sub-brand links in YouTube About** — On The Dax Collective YouTube, list all sub-brand channel links in the About/Links section.
8. **Set Facebook CTA buttons** — Each Facebook page needs a call-to-action button (Watch Video or Follow).
9. **Upload all profile photos and banners** — After assets are created, manually upload to each account.
10. **Verify email on each account** — Required before handles are fully claimed and searchable.
