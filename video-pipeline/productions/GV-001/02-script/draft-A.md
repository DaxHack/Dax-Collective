# GV-001 Draft A — "The Unseen Work"

**Scripture:** Galatians 6:9 (WEB)
**Estimated duration:** ~42 seconds at 110 wpm
**Word count:** 95

## Script

You've been working. You've been praying. You've been faithful.
And nothing has moved.

When you give your best and results stay invisible, it can start to feel like something is wrong.
That you've been missed.

But quiet seasons aren't empty seasons.
Not all growth shows up on schedule.

Galatians 6:9 says: "Let's not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season — if we don't give up."
Not a promised timeline.
Just a steady word: keep going.

Today you don't need a result.
You need the next right step.

Take it.

If someone you know is carrying this, share it.
