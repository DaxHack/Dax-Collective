# GV-001 — Visual Asset Manifest

**Approach:** Original procedural "cinematic stills". Every scene is drawn in code by
`06-render/build_gv001.py` (numpy + Pillow) and has slow camera movement plus live light and particle motion.
No stock footage, no downloaded imagery, no AI-generated images, no faces.

**Why procedural:** the source rule is "public domain or existing repo assets only". The only God's Vessel
images in the repo are merch photos, and the brief rules out forced merchandise references.
Original drawn scenes avoid both the licensing question and the merch problem, and they rank highest on
originality.
