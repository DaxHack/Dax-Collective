**Estimated duration:** ~44 seconds at 110 wpm

## Script

You've been faithful.
Nothing has moved.
That's the honest situation.

It's possible to do everything right — work hard, pray, show up —
and still have nothing to point to.

That's not a failure.
That's what faithfulness often looks like from the inside.

Hebrews 11 describes faith as "assurance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen."
Not proof of results.
Trust in what you can't yet see.

Today's job isn't to see the outcome.
It's to take the next step without one.

Pass this on if someone needs to hear it.
