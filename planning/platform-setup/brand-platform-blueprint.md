## CROSS-PLATFORM BRANDING STANDARDS

These rules apply to ALL five brands.

### Naming Consistency Rule
Every brand's handle must be **identical across all platforms** or differ by a maximum of one separator character (underscore or period). No brand should have wildly different names on different platforms.

### Bio Linking Rule
Every sub-brand bio should reference @thedaxcollective (parent) on at least one platform. The parent brand bio should list all sub-brands.

### Profile Image Rule
- All profile images must be **circle-safe** (main content within center 80% of frame)
- Minimum 1000x1000px, exported as PNG
- Must look clean at 32px (tiny favicon/notification size)

### Color Identity Quick Reference

| Brand | Primary Color | Secondary | Gradient Direction |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-------------------|
| The Dax Collective | Black | Blue/Purple/Pink | L→R rainbow |
| Dax the Traveler | Deep Blue | Purple/Gray | Top→Bottom |
| Time-Zone Travelers | Orange | Deep Red | L→R warm |
| Ani-Dax | Purple | Pink/Blue | L→R vibrant |
| God's Vessel | Indigo/Purple | Deep Blue | Radial from center |

### Thumbnail Style Guide (all brands)
- Always include face/character in thumbnail (boosts CTR)
- Bold, readable text overlay (max 4–5 words)
- High contrast between text and background
- Brand color used as accent or background
- 1280x720px minimum
